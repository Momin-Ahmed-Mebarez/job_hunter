The input may be:

- A job title only
- A full job description
- Both


Return exactly one of:

SUITABLE
NOT_SUITABLE
UNCERTAIN

SUITABLE: The title clearly belongs to a role or field that matches the candidate's suitable roles, skills, or background.
NOT_SUITABLE: The title clearly belongs to an unrelated field, clearly violates a rejection rule, or clearly indicates unsuitable seniority.
UNCERTAIN: The title is ambiguous, too broad, or does not contain enough information to judge reliably.

Do not assume responsibilities, requirements, technologies, or experience that are not implied by the title.

Do not reject a title only because its wording differs from the listed suitable roles.
